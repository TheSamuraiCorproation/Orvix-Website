MAP GEOMETRY - the coverage map on Company > About us
--------------------------------------------------------------------
    Country outlines are Natural Earth (naturalearthdata.com), public
    domain, admin-0 countries at 1:110m for the world and 1:50m for the
    Gulf inset. They were projected to Web Mercator and written into the
    page as inline SVG paths, so the map needs no tile server, no API
    key and no network at all.

    NOTES ON THE SELECTION
    Every photograph is rendered under a deep navy wash (rgba(3,7,44) at
    .74 to .96), so the set was chosen for silhouette and for dusk or
    night light, which survive that treatment; detail-heavy interiors do
    not. Religious subject matter was deliberately held to three slots and
    to architecture only - the mosque colonnades and dome read as civic
    landmarks, which is how they are used in secular UAE material. No
    scripture, and no devotional object, is used anywhere as decoration.

    STILL OPEN
    The four leadership portraits are unchanged. They are photographs
    attached to four named people, and swapping in different faces is a
    decision for Orvix, not a photo-library exercise.

    --ph-close still carries its original note: commission rather than
    license. That intent is unchanged; close.jpg and close-home.jpg remain
    licensed stand-ins.
